3. Memory shortcuts

Early = value-heavyPairs, strong aces, broadways. Avoid weak offsuit aces.
Late = attackCO and BTN are where suited hands, small pairs and weaker aces become opens.
SB = raise more, call lessYou are out of position for the entire hand.
BB = defend wideYou already have 1 BB invested and close the action preflop.
Important: this is a memorization baseline. Open size, rake, stack depth and opponent tendencies can move the boundaries substantially. Against tight players, fold more to aggression. Against loose steals, widen your 3-bets and BB defense.
